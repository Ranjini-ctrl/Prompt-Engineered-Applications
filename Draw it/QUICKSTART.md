# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - Draw It

## Getting Started in 3 Simple Steps!

### Step 1: Open the App
1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. You'll see the beautiful landing page with animations!

### Step 2: Create Your Account
1. Click the **"🎨 Sign Up"** button
2. Fill in:
   - Username (your creative name!)
   - Email (to identify your account)
   - Password (at least 6 characters)
   - Confirm Password
3. **Choose your favorite avatar** (Boy, Girl, Cat, Rabbit, Fox, or Panda)
4. Click **"Create Account 🚀"**
5. Watch the confetti celebration! 🎉

### Step 3: Start Creating!
You'll be redirected to the **Home Page** where you can:
- **Free Drawing** - Create anything you imagine
- **Coloring Book** - Color pre-made templates
- **My Gallery** - View your saved artwork
- **Profile** - Customize your settings

---

## 🎨 Using Free Drawing

1. Click **"Free Drawing"** from home
2. Choose your tool:
   - **🖊️ Pen** - Precise lines
   - **🖌️ Brush** - Soft strokes
   - **✏️ Pencil** - Sketchy lines
   - **🖍️ Marker** - Bold strokes
   - **🌈 Rainbow** - Colorful gradients
   - **🧹 Eraser** - Remove mistakes

3. Adjust **Size** and **Opacity** sliders
4. Pick a color from:
   - Color picker
   - Quick color palette

5. Draw on the canvas!
6. Use buttons:
   - **↶ Undo** - Go back one step
   - **↷ Redo** - Go forward one step
   - **🗑️ Clear** - Start over
   - **💾 Save** - Save to gallery
   - **⬇️ Download** - Download as PNG

### Keyboard Shortcuts:
- `Ctrl/Cmd + Z` - Undo
- `Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Z` - Redo
- `Ctrl/Cmd + S` - Save
- `E` - Eraser tool
- `B` - Brush tool
- `P` - Pen tool

---

## 🖍️ Using Coloring Book

1. Click **"Coloring Book"** from home
2. Choose a category:
   - 🍎 Fruits
   - 🐱 Animals
   - 🚗 Objects
   - 🌹 Flowers
   - 👦 Kids
   - 🎄 Festivals

3. Click any template to open it
4. Use tools:
   - **🖌️ Brush** - Paint with strokes
   - **🪣 Fill** - Fill entire areas (click once)
   - **🧹 Eraser** - Remove color

5. Pick colors and start coloring!
6. **Save** or **Download** when done
7. Click **← Back** to choose another template

---

## 🖼️ My Gallery

1. Click **"My Gallery"** from home
2. View all your saved artwork
3. Click any artwork to:
   - **👁️ View** - See full size
   - **⬇️ Download** - Save to computer
   - **🗑️ Delete** - Remove from gallery

---

## 👤 Profile & Settings

1. Click **"Profile"** from home
2. View your stats:
   - Total drawings count
   - Favorite color
   - Join date

3. **Change Avatar** - Pick a new avatar
4. **Edit Profile** - Update username/email
5. **Settings**:
   - 🌙 Dark Mode - Toggle dark/light theme
   - 🎵 Music - Background music (coming soon)
   - 🔔 Sound Effects - Button sounds
   - 💾 Auto-save - Save every 10 seconds

6. **Logout** - Sign out of your account

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

### For Best Experience:
- Use **Google Chrome** or **Firefox**
- Enable **full screen** for drawing (click ⛶ button)
- Try different **brush sizes** for variety
- Use **Undo** freely - experiment!
- **Save often** - don't lose your work

### Drawing Tips:
- Start with **light colors** for backgrounds
- Use **small brush** for details
- Try the **Rainbow Pen** for fun effects
- Use **Eraser** to fix mistakes
- **Download** your favorites to keep forever

### Coloring Tips:
- Use **Fill tool** for large areas
- Use **Brush** for edges and details
- Try different **color combinations**
- **Save** your colored templates
- Color the same template **different ways**!

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Canvas Not Loading?
- Refresh the page (F5)
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different browser

### Lost Your Drawings?
- Check **My Gallery**
- Don't clear browser data/LocalStorage
- Drawings are saved per browser

### Can't Login?
- Check email and password
- Make sure you signed up first
- Passwords are case-sensitive

### Drawing Lag?
- Close other browser tabs
- Use smaller brush sizes
- Clear old drawings from gallery
- Restart browser
